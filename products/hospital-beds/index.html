Choosing a bed

Share the care requirement first

The right configuration depends on positioning frequency, mobility, caregiver access, room space and clinical advice.

  • Care setting: hospital, nursing facility or home
  • Required movements: backrest, knee-rest, height or tilt
  • Patient mobility and transfer requirements
  • Side rails, wheels, brakes and compatible mattress needs
  • Doorway, lift and room-access measurements
For patient-specific medical advice, consult the treating healthcare professional. Confirm electrical supply, safe working load and manufacturer instructions for the selected model.

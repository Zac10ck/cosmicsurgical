Beds and bed-sore prevention

Water beds, air beds and hospital beds for old patients

For an elderly patient who spends most of the day in bed, the mattress is the most important purchase in the house. Bed sores start within hours; the right bed prevents them.

Read the bed-sore guide →
AIR BED

Alternating-pressure air beds

A pump shifts pressure across the air cells every few minutes so no part of the skin is loaded for long. The standard choice for bedridden patients.

  • Bubble type for moderate risk
  • Tubular type for fully immobile or heavier patients
  • Quiet pumps for night use
  • Sized to standard hospital beds
WATER BED

Water beds for patients

Spreads the patient’s weight across a fluid surface with no pump running. Quiet and comfortable; heavier to move.

  • No electricity needed
  • Good for long-term bedridden care
  • Fits standard and home beds
  • Delivered filled and set up
HOSPITAL BED

Electric & manual hospital beds

Adjustable backrest, knee-rest and height so an elderly caregiver is not lifting a patient by hand ten times a day.

  • 2, 3 and 5-function electric beds
  • Manual fowler and semi-fowler beds
  • Side rails, overbed tables, IV stands
  • Free installation and demonstration
Delivered, installed and demonstrated in the patient’s room — free.
